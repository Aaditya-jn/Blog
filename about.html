02. Principles

How I
Think

01.

Clarity over cleverness

The best solution is the one that is easiest to understand, not the one that is most impressive to write. Complexity should live in the problem, not the solution.

02.

Concrete before abstract

Abstractions should be derived from understanding, not from anticipation. Build the concrete thing first. The right abstraction emerges from seeing the pattern, not imagining it.

03.

Build in public

Sharing unfinished work is the fastest path to understanding what you actually know versus what you think you know. Feedback is not a threat — it is the product.
